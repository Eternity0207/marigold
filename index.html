Marigold

For Día de los Muertos, and every day after

An ofrenda made of their own words.

Bring the messages, letters and cards someone left you. Marigold finds the things they always said, the foods and songs they loved, and builds them an altar. Open AI models do the reading inside this browser tab, so their words never leave your device.

A promise: Marigold will never write as them. Every word on the altar that is theirs, they really said. The AI only helps you find those words, and helps you write about them.
